## 2026

### Electrospun Fields: 3D Nano-Fiber Material Computation as Design Method

**Wai Lok Wan, Ayah Mahmoud, Sergio Mutis, Avantika Velho, Annie Xing, Behnaz Farahi.**
*ACM SIGGRAPH 2026 Art Papers.*

The original paper introducing this platform. Presents (1) a reusable design method for field-conditioned electrospinning, (2) a bio-compatible material catalog defining an operating envelope, (3) a scaffold taxonomy linking geometry to deposition behavior, and (4) the reproducible robotic platform documented in this repository.
